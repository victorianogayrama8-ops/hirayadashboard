## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene, grounded luxury of a coastal boutique retreat. Designed for discerning guests, resort operators, and property concierges, it seamlessly bridges refined organic hospitality with crisp transactional clarity.

The visual mood evokes natural elements: sun-bleached linen fabrics, soft shore sands, woven bamboo architecture, and deep maritime pine waters. The experience is welcoming, tactile, and uncluttered—rejecting the cold, hyper-glossy aesthetic of mass-market booking platforms in favor of warm materiality, deliberate micro-interactions, and editorial poise.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous breathing space across sandy, warm-linen canvas layers.
- Deep sea teal and pine greens serving as grounding anchors for primary calls-to-action and confirmed states.
- Terracotta, amber, and golden highlights guiding selection states, pricing cues, and badges.
- Organic structural boundaries defined by low-contrast parchment outlines rather than harsh dividers or heavy dropshadows.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from shoreline landscapes—deep evergreen pine, coastal tide pools, terracotta sun-baked tiles, and crushed shell sands.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#114B44` / Deep Pine Teal):** Anchors high-priority actions, primary action buttons, focused selectors, and dark branding accents. Provides a secure, trustworthy ground.
- **Secondary (`#C67D58` / Warm Terracotta):** Directs active status, price emphasis, stepper highlights, and featured callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#E59866` / Golden Sand):** Used for subtle attention markers, gentle notification pills, hover states, and celebratory tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDF8F4` to `#F5E9DF`):** 
  - Canvas Root: `#FDF8F4` (Linen Bleach)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Chalk) for elevated modules or `#FBF2EA` (Sandstone Tint) for recessed groups.
  - Neutral Structural Border: `#E8DED3` (Pressed Reed) for soft outlines.
- **Semantic Text:**
  - Headings & Primary Copy: `#1F2421` (Charcoal Pine)
  - Body Text: `#4A4E4D` (Driftwood Gray)
  - Muted Captions & Details: `#7A807C` (Misted Sage)
- **Status & Feedback:**
  - Success / Confirmed: `#1B5E52` / `#E8F3EE`
  - Advisory / Note: `#C67D58` / `#FDF3EC`

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances coastal elegance with transactional utility:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the atmosphere of an authentic boutique escape. Used for main section anchors, resort property names, modal headings, and the total cost display.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its humanist geometry, open counters, and high legibility at micro sizes on mobile screens. It manages metadata, descriptions, pricing tables, stepped navigation, and form inputs.
- **Figures & Numeric Display:** Numerical figures in pricing summaries inherit font weights of 600 or 700 with proportional tabular numbers enabled to maintain alignment across itemized bills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a calm, asymmetrical 12-column responsive layout built to handle booking workflows alongside continuous contextual receipt sidebars.

### Form Factors & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Main interaction space spans 8 columns, while the sticky booking summary / concierge cart occupies the remaining 4 columns. Gutter width remains fixed at `2rem` (`gutter-lg`) with page margins scaling up to `3.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Single column flow with the booking summary collapsing to a floating bottom drawer or docked sub-section. Gutters drop to `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Clean single-column stack. Page margins contract to `1rem` (`margin`) to maximize touch real estate for card tiles and schedule selectors.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Section spacing relies on `space-2xl` (`3.5rem`) for separating multi-step workflows.
- Grouped functional clusters (e.g., date picker inputs, stay selector radios) sit within `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) gaps.
- Internal component padding defaults to `space-md` for cards and `space-sm` vertically for buttons and selectors.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic dropshadows. Depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** and **Tender Reed Outlines**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** `#FDF8F4` sets the base warm linen ground.
- **Level 1 (Card Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` panels or `#FBF2EA` tinted blocks enclosed in a single 1px outline of `#E8DED3`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Selected Elements):** Outlines shift to `#114B44` (or `#C67D58` for highlights) with subtle tinted background shifts (`#F0F7F5` or `#FAF1E8`). A diffuse, colored ambient glow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(17, 75, 68, 0.08)`) is permitted only on elevated floating sidebars and active modal sheets.
- **Recessed Areas (Input Well / Inactive Slots):** Surface `#F5E9DF` with 0px shadow and no border to express carved-in structure.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded radius of `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the smooth craftsmanship of polished timber, reed mats, and rounded stones:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius for content modules, cabana selection tiles, and step wrappers.
- **Controls & Buttons:** `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` for balanced geometric cohesion. Small functional inputs maintain gentle roundness without becoming fully circular or severe.
- **Badges, Pills, and Chips:** Fully rounded pill-shapes (`9999px`) provide high contrast against structured rectangular cards, making operational statuses ("Direct Booking Guaranteed", "Popular", "4 Options Available") quickly scannable.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#114B44`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 1.5rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1B5E52` with micro-scale (`1.01`). Includes right-aligned chevron or arrow.
- **Secondary / Action Select:** Border 1px `#114B44`, text `#114B44`, background transparent. Hover transitions to `#F0F7F5`.
- **Selected State Button:** Background `#114B44`, text `#FFFFFF` with checkmark icon to confirm selection.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Borderless, text `#7A807C` with hover shift to `#114B44` and text underline.

### Selection Cards (Cabanas, Units, Tour Types)
- Enclosed in `#FFFFFF` card with a 1px `#E8DED3` border and `1rem` corner radius.
- Imagery framed at 16:10 aspect ratio with `0.5rem` inner padding or flush top corners.
- Overlaid metadata chips (guest capacity, view type) placed top-left and top-right using semi-transparent frosted white backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
- Selection toggles show a clear shift: border changes to 1.5px `#114B44`, and the selection button flips to the filled pine state.

### Chips & Badges
- **Guarantee / Trust Badges:** Soft pine background `#E2EFEA`, text `#114B44`, font size `0.6875rem`, bold, pill-shaped.
- **Pricing & Promo Tags:** Soft peach background `#FCEFE7`, text `#C67D58`, font size `0.6875rem`, bold.
- **Counter / Stepper Badges:** Solid `#114B44` circle badge with white numerals to denote the step sequence (1, 2, 3...).

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Text Inputs:** Soft sand fill `#FBF2EA`, 1px border `#E8DED3`, `0.5rem` radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state transitions border to `#114B44` with zero ring offset.
- **Quantity Steppers:** Incremental `-` and `+` touch targets enclosed in `#114B44` circular pills with crisp centered numerals.

### Booking Summary / Sticky Concierge Drawer
- Elevated `#FFFFFF` card with soft `#E8DED3` border.
- Header set in Playfair Display with status chip.
- Condensed itemized list with dotted or clean spaced lines separating label and tabular peso currency.
- Prominent callout container for downpayment calculation utilizing `#F0F7F5` with bold pine font and clear remaining balance display.
